# 📖 What is Campus Fix?

> **One line:** Students report broken stuff on campus. Admins fix it. Everyone sees what happened.

---

## 🎯 The problem

Something breaks on campus. A student tells someone. Then…

- ❌ It goes into a WhatsApp group and disappears
- ❌ Nobody knows if anyone is fixing it
- ❌ The same thing gets reported five times
- ❌ Nobody ever finds out if it got fixed

**Reporting is easy. Knowing what happened next is the broken part.**

---

## ✅ The fix

Every report becomes a **tracked ticket** with:

- 🔖 A reference code — like `#K2HDF6`
- 📍 A location on campus
- 📸 A photo
- 🚦 A live status you can check anytime

---

## 👥 Who uses it

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| 👨‍🎓 **Student** | Reports problems, tracks their own |
| 🛠️ **Admin** | Sees everything, moves reports along, manages the campus setup |

Only two roles. That's on purpose.

---

## 🔄 How a report moves

```
📝 Reported  →  👤 Assigned  →  🔧 In Progress  →  ✅ Fixed
                                                   ↘ ❌ Rejected
```

- **Reported** — filed, waiting to be picked up
- **Assigned** — someone is now responsible
- **In Progress** — work has actually started
- **Fixed** — done and checked
- **Rejected** — not a real issue (with a reason, always)

Every single change is **timestamped and signed** by whoever made it.

## 📱 Built for phones

Students use phones on mobile data. So:

- No heavy 3D
- No web fonts to download
- Loads fast on a cheap Android
- Big tap targets, bottom tab bar
